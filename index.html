Our philosophy

Looking expensive shouldn't be expensive.

We started The Common Athlete with a simple frustration: in Singapore, activewear that feels beautiful costs S$90 a piece — and activewear that's affordable feels like it. We refused to accept that trade-off.

So we design every piece the way premium houses do — dense buttery fabrics, sculpting waistbands, seams placed where they flatter — then sell it directly to you, without the boutique markup. Same factories-grade specification. A fraction of the price.

Our mission: to make every woman in Singapore feel like the best-dressed person in the studio — without thinking twice about the price tag.

The craft

Why it feels
expensive

"Premium" isn't a mood — it's a specification. These are the details we hold every production run to. They're usually invisible. You'll feel every one of them.

Feel it first — join the waitlist
  • 01

    Dense, buttery fabric

    250–280gsm nylon-spandex with a matte, peach-soft hand feel — the same weight class used by brands charging three times more. Dense enough to sculpt, never shiny, never flimsy.

  • 02

    Squat-proof, verified

    Every fabric batch passes an opacity-under-stretch test before it's cut. If it doesn't survive a deep squat under studio lights, it doesn't ship.

  • 03

    Seams where they flatter

    Flatlock stitching for zero chafe, no front seam on our V-waist short, and gusseted construction throughout. Clean lines on you, not just on the rack.

  • 04

    No-roll waistbands

    High, sculpting waistbands engineered to stay put through burpees, brunches and bus rides. Tested stretched and relaxed, to the centimetre.

  • 05

    Tagless everything

    Heat-transfer labels instead of itchy tags, bonded edges at necklines and armholes, bartacked stress points. Comfort you notice by never noticing.
